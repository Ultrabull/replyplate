The chat helper, working

This page is pretending to be a restaurant's website. The button in the corner is the thing. Try it.

Ask it these:
  • Are you open on Sunday?
  • Do you have vegan options?
  • Is there parking?
  • Can I bring my dog?
  • Do you take bookings for 10 people?
  • Then try something odd, like "do you sell bicycles"

Why a restaurant wants this

They get asked the same eight questions all day, usually by phone, usually in the middle of service. This answers them at 11pm on a Sunday without anyone picking up.

It cannot make anything up.

There is no AI inside this widget. It only ever repeats an answer the owner wrote. Ask it something it wasn't told and it says so, then gives you the phone number.

That matters most for allergies. A chatbot that guesses "yes, that's gluten free" is not an embarrassment, it is a hospital visit and a lawsuit in the restaurant's name. This one physically cannot do it.

It is one small file.

No accounts, no monthly fee to anyone else, nothing to log into. It loads instantly and works on a phone. The restaurant pastes two lines into their site and it is done.

What the restaurant pastes in

Two lines, just before </body> on their site. The answers are written into it, so nothing is fetched from anywhere.
