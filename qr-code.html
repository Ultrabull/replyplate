2. The words on it

Short wins. People read a card for about two seconds.

None of these can be conditional. Not if you enjoyed it, not if we earned 5 stars, and never a discount for reviewing. Google prohibits asking only your happy customers, and the penalty lands on your listing. Whatever you write has to read the same to somebody who had a bad night.

3. What to print

All of these fit a normal US Letter sheet on an ordinary printer.

SVG stays sharp at any size, so that is the one to send a print shop. PNG is the one for anywhere that wants a picture file.

How big should it be? Print the code at least an inch across, and bigger wherever people stand further away. Leave the white border alone: scanners need that space to find the code. Never stretch it out of square.
