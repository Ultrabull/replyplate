Careful with the words "No fee"

That means no fee to the diner. It says nothing about what you pay. A pickup order through a marketplace app still costs the restaurant a commission, commonly somewhere around 6% to 15% depending on the plan, and a delivery order a good deal more. What we keep up on it

A reply under every review

Written in your words, and anything negative waits for your OK before it goes anywhere near your listing. It shows as Response from the owner, in your restaurant's name, never ours.

Your own ordering link in that list

Google lets you add it free, and mark it the one you would rather they used. If you have no website we host the page for you, so the link works from day one.

Posts, so it is not three years old

Offers and news on the listing itself, where somebody is already looking you up. Every one checked against the rules Google rejects on before it reaches you.

Hours that are actually right

Including the week you shut in September. A listing that says Open when the door is locked is the one-star you did not need to earn.

One thing we will not tell you: that any of this moves your rating or your position in search. Nobody can promise that. What it changes is what a stranger finds when they get there.
