# When they cannot add code to their website

Some restaurants cannot paste your two lines. A free Wix site, a Canva page, a
Squarespace Personal plan, or nothing but an Instagram account. This is what
you offer them, and it is close to everything.

---

## First, the thing that makes this a small problem

There are four ways a diner reaches the ordering. **Only one of them needs
code.**

| How they get there | Needs code? |
|---|---|
| Taps **Order online** on the Google listing | **No** |
| Scans a **QR code** on the table, the counter or the bag | **No** |
| Taps the link in the **Instagram bio** | **No** |
| Taps the **chat bubble on the restaurant's own website** | **Yes** |

Three of the four are a link. A link works everywhere, on every platform, free
or paid, forever.

So a restaurant that cannot paste code loses the bubble in the corner of their
own site. They keep Google, they keep the QR codes, they keep Instagram. And
Google is the biggest of the four by a distance, because it is where people
look for dinner.

---

## What everybody gets, code or no code

Their own page, hosted by you:

```
reply-plate.com/r.html?c=their-name
```

Their menu, the chat, and pickup ordering. You build it from the console:
**Website chat → Or: no code at all → Copy the page file**, then add it to the
site as `r/their-name.json`.

That one link then goes in four places:

1. **Their Google listing**, under Food ordering, marked preferred
2. **A QR code** for table cards, takeout bags and the counter
3. **Their Instagram bio**
4. **A button on their existing website**

That fourth one surprises people. **Adding a link is not adding code.** Every
website builder on earth lets you put a button on a page and point it
somewhere, including the free plans. So even a free Wix site can have a big
**Order for pickup** button that opens their ordering page.

---

## Adding that button, per platform

Say this to the owner, or do it for them on a screen share.

**Wix, any plan:** Add → Button → change the text to "Order for pickup" → Link
→ Web address → paste the link → set it to open in a new tab.

**Squarespace, any plan:** Edit the page → add a Button block → text "Order for
pickup" → paste the link.

**Canva Websites:** add a rectangle or a text box, select it, use the link tool
in the toolbar, paste the link.

**GoDaddy, Weebly, Square Online:** all have a Button element. Same three steps.

**Instagram:** Edit profile → Links → Add link → paste it → title it "Order for
pickup".

**Facebook page:** Edit action button → choose Order Food or Start Order →
paste the link.

Put the button near the top of their home page and again at the bottom. Do not
bury it under the menu.

## The ladder, in the order to offer it

**1. The link. Free, included, works today.**
Everything above. This is not a downgrade, it is the same product reached a
different way. Do not apologise for it.

**2. They want the chat on their own site.**
Two honest routes, and it is their choice:

- **Upgrade their plan.** Wix is about $17 a month for the tier that allows
  custom code, Squarespace Business about $23. That money goes to Wix or
  Squarespace, not to you. Say that out loud, it builds trust.
- **We build you one instead.** $1,200 once, and it comes with the chat and the
  ordering already in it, no monthly fee for the site itself. For a restaurant
  paying Wix $17 a month for a site they do not like, this is often the easier
  decision, and it is worth doing that sum with them.

**3. They have no website at all.**
Their hosted page is their website for now. It has their menu, their hours,
their phone number and their ordering, at a real address they can print. When
they are ready for a proper one, you build it.

## What to say when they ask

**"Will this work on my website?"**

> Three of the four ways people order need nothing from your website at all.
> The Google one is the big one, and it works whatever your site is built on.
> If you want the chat bubble on your own pages too, that depends on your
> website plan, and I will check it for you in a minute.

**"So I am getting less than other restaurants?"**

> No. Same menu, same ordering, same chat, same phone. The only difference is
> whether it sits inside your website or one tap away from it. Most of your
> orders will come from Google either way.

**"Why can't you just make it work?"**

> Wix and Squarespace only allow outside code on their paid plans. It is their
> rule and nobody can get round it. That is why we host the page for you
> instead, so you are not paying anybody extra just to be allowed.

---

## Two things not to do

**Do not tell them to switch website platform.** Moving a restaurant's website
is a week of their life and it will be your fault when their Google listing
breaks. The hosted link avoids the whole problem.

**Do not quietly sell the $1,200 site as the fix.** Lead with the free route
that works today. Offer the build when they ask for the bubble, not before.
Selling a website to somebody who came for review replies is how you lose the
$199 as well.
