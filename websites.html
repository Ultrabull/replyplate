📸Your food, not stock photos

Before you ask

It does not take card payments. People order and pay when they pick up, or on the phone. That means no card fees, no refunds to process, and no chargebacks landing on you six weeks later.

It is one good page, not ten. A restaurant needs the food, the hours, the address and a way to reach you. Ten pages is somewhere for information to go stale.

It does not replace DoorDash or Uber Eats. Those apps have people on them who have never heard of you. Keep them. This is for the people who already searched your name, where you keep the whole ticket.

You own it. The domain goes in your name, not mine, and the files are yours. If you leave, the site keeps working and you can hand it to anyone.

Already have a site? Often the cheaper answer is fixing that one. Send me the link and I will tell you straight which of the two is the better spend, including when it is neither.

It takes about a week. Most of that is waiting on your photos and your menu. The building is the fast part.
