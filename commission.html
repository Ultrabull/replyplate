A commission does not dent your margin on those orders. It removes it. You cook the food, you buy the ingredients, you pay the kitchen, and the order roughly breaks even.

That is fine for a stranger. The apps put you in front of people who have never heard of you, and finding new customers is worth paying for.

It is not fine for a regular. Somebody who searched your name, on Google, because they already wanted your food, and then tapped DoorDash because that was the only button there. You paid a finder's fee to be found by somebody who had already found you.
