Worth knowing
No app, ever

It opens in the browser their phone already has. Asking a customer to install something for a $9 lunch is how you lose the sale.

The card never touches your site

Screen three is Stripe's own page on Stripe's own address. Neither your website nor mine ever sees a card number, which is the only reason this is safe to build at all.

Paying is what unlocks knowing

The screen that tells them what to grab only appears after payment. That single ordering rule does more against walkouts than any camera.
