Worth knowing
Your staff never look at the customer's phone

The order is on the counter screen because Stripe said it was paid, not because someone held up a screen. A screenshot of a friend's confirmation gets nobody anything, and staff never have to judge whether a phone is showing something real.

The food in their hand wins

The list does not stop them adding a third samosa when the shelf thinks there are two. They are holding it. Refusing the payment for something already in their hands is the one thing that would make this worse than a till.

No app, ever

It opens in the browser their phone already has. Asking a customer to install something for a $9 lunch is how you lose the sale.

The card never touches your site

Screen three is Stripe's own page on Stripe's own address. Neither your website nor mine ever sees a card number, which is the only reason this is safe to build at all. Stripe emails the receipt too.
