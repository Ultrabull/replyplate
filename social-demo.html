The part that matters: it gets checked first

Google rejects posts, quietly, and an owner rarely finds out why. Every post is run against the rules before it is handed over. This is what a bad one looks like.

BEST PIZZA IN PORTLAND!! Call us on (503) 555 0142 to book your table!! Visit www.theolivetable.com for the full menu!!
Do not post this yet
  • There is a phone number in the body. Google rejects posts for this. Use the Call now button instead. (503) 555 0142
  • Repeated punctuation. Google lists extra characters as a reason to reject.
  • There is a link in the body. Put it on the button instead, where it is clickable.
  • A superlative claim. Keep it to what the restaurant can stand behind.
  • No date or day in the text. Offers need start and end dates in Google's form, and saying it in the words too helps.
