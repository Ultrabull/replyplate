What to look for (60 seconds on their Google listing)
  1. Star rating and review count. 3.8 to 4.4 with plenty of reviews is the sweet spot. They care, and there's room.
  2. Scroll the recent reviews. How many have a reply from the owner? That's your opening.
  3. Find the newest reply. "March" or "never" is the line that lands.
  4. Copy one review, ideally a bad one. You'll send them a reply to it, written for free.
  5. Check their Instagram or Facebook. When was the last post?
