How it works

  1. One sticker, two places. One by the walk-in, one at the unit. The helper who moves the stock is the only person who taps, so it is one person's habit, not everybody's.
  2. Three buttons, nothing else. A delivery came in. Some went to the kitchen. The kitchen used it. That is the whole job.
  3. You get told which problem you have. Low in the kitchen with stock at the unit means send someone, and costs nothing. Low in both means order. Never the same sentence for both.

Worth knowing

A nudge, not an order

You are told what crossed the line you set, and the amount to bring it back. Nothing is ordered for you. You still make the call.

Only as true as the last tap

Miss a tap and the number drifts until somebody counts. That is what the count is for, and the gap it finds is exactly the stock that moved without anyone saying so.

Your places, your amounts

A garage, a lock-up, a cousin's cold room, whatever you actually use. You choose what goes on the list and what counts as low, for each place.
