What this is, plainly

No counting, only in and out

Nobody types a full stock take. A number goes up when something arrives, and down when something is taken. That is the whole entry.

A nudge, not an order

When something crosses the line you set, you are told and told the amount to bring it back. Nothing is ordered for you. You still make the call.

Only as true as the last scan

If somebody takes stock without scanning, the number is wrong until the next one. That is on the habit in the kitchen, not on the code.
