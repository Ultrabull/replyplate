Three rules this report follows

Every number is countable

The reviews it was built from are in the box above. If it says four people mentioned waiting, you can find those four. Nothing is rounded up to sound more useful.

Patterns, not just topics

"People mentioned waiting" is not worth reading. "Three of the four were Friday or Saturday evening" tells an owner where to stand next week.

No promises about your rating

It will never tell you a score is about to go up. Nobody can know that, and any tool that says otherwise is guessing at your expense.
