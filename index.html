Before you buy, the honest version

Every diner, not just the happy ones. Everyone sees the same QR code. Filtering to only the happy ones breaks Google's rules, and the FTC treats review suppression as deceptive, so we will not do it.

We will not promise a better rating. Nobody honest will. Google will even draft replies free on some accounts and the writing is fine, but it still needs you to log in and press send, every time, forever.

Google reviews only. Not Yelp, not TripAdvisor, not OpenTable. Facebook retired star ratings, so no tool can reply to those. We do handle your Facebook and Instagram posts.

Anything negative waits for you. It reaches your phone with a calm reply already written and waits. There is no setting that turns this off. Anything already posted can be edited or deleted from your Google profile at any time, by you or by us.

AI writes the first version. A person checks it. Nothing posts under an invented employee name. Replies go out as your restaurant, because that is whose name is on them.

One person, and much of it by hand. ReplyPlate is run by one person, and today a good part of the work is done by hand. Your reviews get worked every business day, not within a minute of landing.

We will not text your diners. Texting strangers in the US carries damages of hundreds of dollars a message. We ask with a printed code instead, and a code left in the office drawer gets you nothing, which is why we push receipts first.

If you leave, you keep the work. Every reply we posted stays up, in your name, and we never take them down. Your review code keeps working forever, because it points at Google and not at us. We come off your Google profile the same day you say so. Nothing you paid for gets switched off behind you.

If $199 is the difference between tight and fine this month, don't buy it. It will still be $199 in the autumn, and that is our real price, not an introductory rate.
